Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DietApp ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information we collect

When you use DietApp, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your email address and a securely hashed password, or — if you sign in with Google or Apple — the identifier and email provided by that service.
  • Profile information: height, weight, birth date, gender, activity level, and your dietary goal, which you provide during onboarding to calculate your personalized calorie and macronutrient targets.
  • Diet diary data: the foods and quantities you log, including any custom foods you create.
  • Plate photos (optional, premium feature): if you use the AI photo-analysis feature, the photo you take or select is sent to our backend and forwarded to our AI provider (OpenAI) solely to estimate the calorie and macronutrient content of your meal. The photo itself is not permanently stored on our servers — only the resulting text estimate (description, calories, protein, carbs, fat) is saved to your diary.
  • Technical information: basic request metadata (such as IP address) needed to operate the service securely, including issuing and refreshing your sign-in session.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To calculate your personalized daily calorie and macronutrient targets.
  • To let you log meals and track your progress over time.
  • To provide AI-estimated nutrition values from a plate photo, when you choose to use that feature.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

3. Third-party services

We share the minimum data necessary with the following third parties:

  • OpenAI — receives the plate photo you submit (only when you use the AI photo-analysis feature) to generate a nutrition estimate. OpenAI processes this data under its own API data-usage policies.
  • Google / Apple — if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, those providers authenticate you and share your basic profile (email, name) with us to create your account.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and diary data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete my account inside the app. This action is irreversible and removes your profile, nutrition goals, diary history, and AI analysis history from our systems.

5. Data security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored in plain text. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to production data is restricted.

6. Children's privacy

DietApp is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and onboarding requires an age between 13 and 100. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your rights

You can access and edit most of your data directly within the app (profile, goals, diary entries). You may request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us at the email address below, in addition to the in-app deletion option.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us
